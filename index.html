Javier Fajardo

Researcher in Environmental Conservation at DICE (University of Kent). Javier Fajardo

I am a conservation scientist studying how area-based conservation can protect biodiversity while advancing equity. My research focuses on conservation planning, conservation effectiveness, and data justice in biodiversity policy.

Research

My work asks how conservation, particularly global biodiversity commitments to expand protected and conserved areas, can become effective and equitable conservation in practice. I examine how planning choices, the data that are available or missing, and the ways affected people and places are represented shape conservation outcomes across different ecological and social contexts.

Research projects

Advancing effective and equitable OECMs at scale in Southeast Asia

In my new postdoctoral position within the E3 “Sharing Space for Nature” project at the University of Kent, I will examine how, where, and under what conditions conserved areas (i.e., other effective area-based conservation measures or OECMs) can deliver ecological and social outcomes across multi-use landscapes and seascapes.
